Project Overview

The system is designed to help lecturers easily submit, manage, and monitor their proposals through a structured and user-friendly digital platform. SIPMAS simplifies administrative processes, minimizes paperwork, and improves efficiency in proposal handling.

This project demonstrates the implementation of role-based access, data management, and a clean workflow to support academic community service activities effectively.

Key Features

SIPMAS - Research & Community Service Management System

SIPMAS is a web-based information system developed using the Laravel framework to manage research and community service activities within an academic institution. The system is designed to simplify the submission, monitoring, and evaluation process of research and community service proposals.

SIPMAS provides a centralized dashboard where administrators and reviewers can efficiently manage submitted proposals. Users can filter proposals by title, scheme, proposer name, year, and status, enabling fast and accurate data retrieval. The system also displays statistical summary cards that present the total number of proposals based on each research or community service scheme, offering a quick overview of proposal distribution.

Each proposal is displayed in a structured table containing essential information such as proposal title, scheme, team members, submission year, and current status. Users can access detailed proposal information, track submission progress, and review supporting documents directly through the system.

SIPMAS implements role-based access control, allowing different user roles such as administrators, reviewers, and lecturers to access features according to their responsibilities. Administrators can manage users, schemes, and reviewer assignments, while reviewers can evaluate proposals and provide assessments. Lecturers can submit proposals and monitor their progress transparently.

By digitizing and automating the proposal management workflow, SIPMAS improves efficiency, reduces administrative workload, and ensures a structured and transparent research and community service management process.

Tech Stack: Laravel, PHP, MySQL, Blade Template, Bootstrap
