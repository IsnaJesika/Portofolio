Monster Ball

2D Game
Multiplayer

Game Overview

Monster Ball is an exciting two-player game designed for competitive gameplay. Each player controls their own character in an arena filled with challenges and obstacles. The game combines strategy, timing, and quick reflexes to create an engaging multiplayer experience.

Gameplay Mechanics

  • Objective:
    • Players compete to score points by hitting the ball past their opponent's boundary
    • First player to reach 5 points wins the match
    • Fast-paced action requires quick decision-making and precise control
  • Controls:
    • Move your hero up and down to position yourself
    • Jump diagonally to reach higher positions
    • Time your movements to hit the ball effectively
    • Use strategic positioning to outmaneuver your opponent
  • Features:
    • Smooth character animations and physics
    • Real-time competitive gameplay
    • Score tracking system
    • Multiple character options
